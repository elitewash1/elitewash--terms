1. General Disclaimer

EliteWash takes reasonable care and precaution while handling, cleaning and processing all garments. However, certain risks are inherent to garment cleaning due to variations in fabric composition, garment condition, age, quality, colour stability and manufacturing characteristics.

EliteWash shall not be held responsible for damage, colour fading, shrinkage, fabric deterioration, loss of shape or damage to embellishments arising from the inherent characteristics or pre-existing condition of garments, subject to applicable laws.

2. Colour Bleeding & Fabric Damage Policy

Customers are responsible for selecting an appropriate cleaning service based on the garment's fabric type, care label and special handling requirements.

  • Regular laundry processes may not be suitable for delicate, sensitive or colour-sensitive garments.
  • Colour bleeding, colour transfer, fading, shrinkage and fabric damage may occur during washing, particularly with garments having unstable dyes or sensitive fabrics.
  • Delicate, expensive, silk, woollen, embellished or colour-sensitive garments should be submitted under an appropriate specialised cleaning service wherever recommended by their care instructions.
  • Customers must inform our staff about known colour instability, fabric sensitivities, existing damage or special care requirements at the time of submission.

3. Daily Wear Laundry Services Disclaimer

This policy applies to all regular laundry services, including Wash & Fold, Wash & Steam Iron, Woollen Laundry and other standard washing services.

Regular laundry services may involve machine washing, water-based cleaning, detergents, mechanical agitation, rinsing and drying processes. The results may vary depending on the fabric composition, colour, quality, age and existing condition of each garment.

Customers are advised to understand the following:

  • Regular washing may result in colour bleeding, colour transfer, fading, shrinkage, stretching, fabric deterioration, pilling or loss of garment shape.
  • Prints, embroidery, embellishments, buttons, decorative elements and delicate stitching may be affected during regular laundry processing.
  • Delicate, expensive, silk, woollen, colour-sensitive and special-care garments should not be submitted under regular laundry services unless their care labels permit the selected cleaning method.
  • Garments requiring specialised treatment should be submitted under an appropriate service, such as professional dry cleaning or specialised fabric care, wherever suitable.
  • Customers are responsible for checking garment care labels and informing EliteWash of any special washing instructions or known fabric-related concerns before processing.

By selecting a regular laundry service, customers acknowledge that the selected cleaning process carries certain inherent fabric-related risks. EliteWash will exercise reasonable care while processing all garments.

4. Stain Removal Policy

Stain removal is not guaranteed under any laundry or dry cleaning service.

EliteWash makes reasonable efforts to treat stains using appropriate cleaning methods. However, certain stains, including oil, ink, dye, chemical, protein-based and old or previously treated stains, may be permanent or may not be completely removed.

Stain treatment may also carry a risk of colour alteration or fabric damage, depending on the nature and condition of the garment.

Dry cleaning is performed using suitable professional cleaning methods according to the garment's fabric type and condition.

Although reasonable care is taken during the process, stain removal and complete restoration of garments cannot be guaranteed.

EliteWash shall not be held responsible for pre-existing fabric defects, weakened fibres, unstable dyes, shrinkage, colour changes or damage resulting from the inherent characteristics or prior condition of garments, subject to applicable laws.

6. Rewash & Complaint Policy

  • Any complaints relating to stains, damage or service quality must be reported within 45 days from the date of delivery.
  • Requests for rewash or reprocessing submitted after 45 days from delivery will not be entertained.
  • Garments that have been worn, used, altered or otherwise put to use after delivery will not be eligible for complimentary rewash.
  • All complaints will be reviewed and assessed by EliteWash before any appropriate resolution is offered.

The maximum compensation under this policy shall not exceed 10 times the service billing value of the affected article, subject to applicable consumer protection laws and legal requirements.
